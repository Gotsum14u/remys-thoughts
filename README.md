Remy's Thoughts
Meet Remy, a big-headed, slightly suspicious black pug with very strong opinions about snacks, naps, and the mailman. Tap him to wake him up, then let him share his thoughts out loud or ask him a question by voice or text. Remy barks, grumbles, and answers in his own deep little voice, with a 3D scene that shows off soft lighting, shadows, and a wagging tail.
Features
A 3D pug you can tap, with blinking, breathing, head tilts, and a wagging tail
Random thoughts that pop up on their own, or on demand with the Thought button
Ask Remy anything by typing or speaking with the Mic button
Spoken replies using your phone's built-in text-to-speech
Synthesized barks and sound effects, with no audio files to download
Installs to your home screen and works offline after the first load
Who it's for
Dog lovers, pug fans, and anyone who wants a little comic company on their phone.
Short version (for app stores or taglines):
"Remy the black pug has thoughts. Tap him, and he'll tell you all of them."
